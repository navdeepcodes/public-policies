Account Deletion – TRUEKINN
Users can request deletion of their TRUEKINN account and associated data by following the steps below.

Steps to request deletion:
1. Send an email from your registered email address to: support@truekinn.app
2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
3. Include your registered email ID in the message.

What data will be deleted:
- User account (Firebase Authentication)
- Profile information (name, bio, college, profile photo)
- Posts, comments, messages, and activity data

What data may be retained:
- Anonymized moderation or legal logs (if required by law), retained for up to 90 days.

Deletion timeline:
- Requests are processed within 7 days.
