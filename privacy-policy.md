# Privacy Policy for Truekinn

Truekinn respects your privacy.

## Data We Collect
- Name, email, and profile information provided by users
- Images uploaded by users (profile photos, posts)
- Usage data for app functionality

- To authenticate users
- To improve app experience

## Data Sharing
We do not sell user data.
Data is shared only with essential services (Firebase, Supabase).

## Data Deletion
Users can request deletion of their account and data.
